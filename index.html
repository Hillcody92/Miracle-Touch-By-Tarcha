The Woman Behind
the Miracle

I am a Master Braider with more than a decade of experience. With a laser focus on quality and the health of your hair, I have perfected the art of braiding. My journey began in my early teens, braiding my own hair before expanding to serve clients.

Today, I proudly own and operate Miracle Touch By Tarcha, a brand that reflects my unwavering commitment to excellence. Allow me to showcase my blessed hands and transform your hair into a true masterpiece.
